JATAYU

Mythological Role: According to the Aranya-Kanda of the Ramayana, Jatayu was a noble bird, son of Aruna and nephew of Garuda, and a dear friend of Rama's father, Dasharatha. The Valiant Fight: Upon hearing Sita’s cries, an elderly Jatayu fought Ravana in the sky, destroying his chariot before being incapacitated by the demon king. The Sacrifice: After being struck down, Jatayu hung on to life to inform Rama of Sita's abduction and the direction Ravana took, leading to Rama performing his final funeral rites. Legacy and Symbolism: Jatayu symbolizes devotion, selfless service to God, and the courage to fight injustice, regardless of the odds. World's Largest Bird Sculpture: Located in Chadayamangalam, Kerala, this center hosts the world's largest functional bird sculpture, honoring Jatayu's sacrifice. Dimensions: The concrete sculpture is 200 feet long, 150 feet wide, and 70 feet tall. Attractions: The site includes a 6D theater, adventure activities (rock climbing, zip-lining), and a museum, blending mythology with sustainable tourism.
