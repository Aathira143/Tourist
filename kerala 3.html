PADMANABHA SWAMY
The Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, is widely regarded as the world's richest temple
due to its vast subterranean vaults containing gold, jewels, and ancient artifacts. Dedicated to Lord Vishnu in the Anantha Shayana
(reclining) posture on the serpent Adi Shesha, it is one of the 108 Divya Desams (sacred abodes of Vishnu).
