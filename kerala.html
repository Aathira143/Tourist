KERALA

Kerala, "God's Own Country," offers diverse attractions, including the tea gardens of Munnar,
serene backwaters of Alleppey, and pristine beaches like Varkala. Key destinations include the
wildlife-rich Thekkady, charming Kochi, adventurous Wayanad, and cultural spots like
Thiruvananthapuram. The best time to visit is August to March.

TOURISTS PLACE
Athirappilly Falls, a 24-meter (80-foot) cascade on the Chalakudy River in Kerala, known as the "Niagara of India,"
remained largely unexplored until the 1950s when roads were built for upstream dams. It is a vital, biodiverse ecosystem
protecting four endangered hornbill species. The area is heavily featured in Indian cinema.
