DAM

Idukki Dam: A 168.91-meter-high double-curvature arch dam across the Periyar River. It is one of Asia's highest arch dams and crucial for hydroelectric power. Banasura Sagar Dam: Located in Wayanad, this is the largest earthen dam in India and second largest in Asia, popular for speed boating and trekking. Mullaperiyar Dam: A 129-year-old masonry gravity dam on the Periyar River. Though located in Kerala, it is operated by Tamil Nadu, often causing inter-state debate over its safety. Malampuzha Dam: Located in Palakkad, it is a major dam providing irrigation and drinking water. Cheruthoni Dam: Located close to the Idukki Arch Dam, it is a key component of the Idukki Hydroelectric Project. Hydroelectric Power & Irrigation: Many dams are designed for both, with the Idukki and Idamalayar dams accounting for nearly 48% of the state's total water storage capacity. Tourism: Dams like Thenmala (Ecotourism), Mangalam, and Vazhani are developed as popular tourist spots with boating and parks. Storage: Kerala's dams are frequently monitored by the Kerala State Electricity Board (KSEB) and the Kerala Irrigation Department.
